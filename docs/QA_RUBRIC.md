# QA Rubric

Apply to every generated variant; report `pass`, `fail`, `blocked`, or `not-run` with evidence paths. Structural checks and visual review are separate.

1. **Provenance and facts:** all factual slides map valid source IDs and locators; unknown IDs fail; unverified evidence remains visibly unverified; plotted values equal deterministic source transforms; no generated claim is presented as verified.
2. **Rights and privacy:** each nontrivial asset has source, creator, license/permission and review state; private inputs remain local unless explicitly authorized; no paid request without configuration and approval.
3. **Structure and editability:** record slide count; text/shape/chart/image counts; native chart workbook/value comparison; notes; editable level per output/element; raster fallback proportion. File extension alone proves nothing.
4. **Visual quality:** render all pages at fixed size/font environment; inspect every page for clipping, overlap, contrast, label readability, font substitution, hierarchy and consistency. Store renderer/version and page images. Automated geometry does not replace visual review.
5. **Narrative/accessibility:** one main assertion per factual slide, concise support, readable type, meaningful chart labels/units, chart description and usable speaker notes; check requested duration against supplied script and rate. A formal Harness deck plan must specify audience, purpose, language, style, ordered content priorities, sections and prohibited copy patterns; its first slide or second after an optional cover must be an agenda listing every section, each section must have one titled transition before its content, and every content slide must map to a declared section. Each slide must declare role, importance, explanation goal, visual rationale and layout intent.
6. **Reproducibility:** record tool/runtime versions, configuration hash, inputs/outputs and SHA-256, transformation, warnings, duration, retries and cost. Re-run with same inputs and compare deterministic outputs where supported.
7. **Repair:** findings identify `slide_id` and `element_id`; only impacted artifacts are rebuilt; regression-check unaffected pages and retain before/after evidence.

No aggregate score may conceal a failed hard constraint. Missing external executable or authorized account is `blocked`, not pass.

## Formal Harness page review

Formal deck completion requires a human reviewer to record a note for every page under each of these checks: content density, typography, visual support, diagram logic, narrative role, presentation voice, language clarity, layout variety, caveat relevance, emphasis/depth allocation, and rendered fit. `not_applicable` requires a reason. Missing checks or unresolved page findings block approval. These records are reviewer attestations bound to the rendered deck hash; they do not prove reviewer identity, taste, or truthfulness, and automated geometry/OCR still cannot certify aesthetic quality or narrative adequacy.

The formal Harness path also runs a deterministic hard gate against the actual PPTX QA manifest and rendered pages. A version 1.1.0 plan must declare talk duration, recognized slide importance levels, and minimum core/high page and speaking-time shares (at least 25% and 50%, respectively). On the full-deck run it blocks a missing or incomplete agenda, missing or misnamed section transitions, unassigned content slides, section-transition copy with fewer than 18 visible text units beyond its heading, text outside bounds, any detected text-box overlap, authored text below 12 pt even in the footer area, authored text whose font size cannot be measured from explicit OOXML runs, missing page images, visible content below the planned minimum explanation length, body-text occupancy below 4.5% on visual pages or 6% on text-only pages, fewer than two rendered body-text lines, materialized-visual coverage below the plan's minimum ratio, a planned visual missing from its page, recognized assistant-to-user phrasing in visible copy or speaker notes, long English-only blocks in a Chinese deck, five consecutive content pages with the same composition signature, fewer than 40% distinct composition signatures across five or more content pages, one composition signature covering at least four pages and at least 60% of content pages, three or more disclaimer-style clauses on one content page, the same disclaimer repeated on three or more content pages, core/high pages below the declared share, and core/high talk time below the declared share. Layout signatures use slide type, structured element kinds, and coarse rendered body-object distribution when available; they ignore plan-authored `layout_intent`. Representative-slide pilots defer the full-deck diversity check. The voice screen covers common Chinese and English direct-address and assistant-narration phrases; it is a pattern screen, so unusual paraphrases may escape it and an unfortunate phrase may need editorial review. When slide durations are estimated, recognized importance levels adjust the allocation; authored durations remain unchanged. A single specific caveat remains allowed. The caveat phrase screen catches common English and Chinese forms; it cannot decide whether one caveat is relevant or whether differently worded caveats repeat the same idea. OCR failures are reported as warnings for human inspection rather than treated as proof of clipping. The gate cannot choose the right importance labels or establish that the core pages explain a topic well; those decisions remain in the approved plan and human `emphasis_depth` review.
